# AI Usage shows provider icons and omits plans

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Type:** View presentation
- **Supersedes:** —
- **Superseded by:** —

## Decision

Show the Claude and Codex brand marks beside their provider names in the AI
Usage view. Codex accounts share the OpenAI mark. Omit subscription plan
labels. Keep cached-data and provider-error notices.

## Context

Provider names alone take longer to identify at a glance. Subscription tiers
add text without helping the display communicate remaining quota.

## Why

Brand marks make the providers easier to recognize. Plan information remains
accepted by the channel contract for compatibility, but does not occupy the
panel header.

## Evidence

Owner, CastKit AI Usage thread, 2026-10-05 (chat ID unavailable in runtime):

> We're missing the Claude and Codex icons. It would make this easier to eyeball.
> We don't need the plan they're on via CastKit though. Not important.
