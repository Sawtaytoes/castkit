CastKit — an image on one side, the time and agenda on the other

Five layouts, drawn at each panel's real framebuffer size, in the real panel inks (Spectra 6 resolves white to #D0D2D2 and the accent to #1F1EAF) and the real panel typeface (Atkinson Hyperlegible). Photos are the repo's committed CC0 fixtures. The split point is measured on the visible window, and the photo still bleeds to the panel edge under the mat — the rule Photo Frame (Duo) already follows. Nothing is dithered here; this is about layout, not ink simulation.

Status: DECIDED on 2026-09-14. Option 1 won, with a paper rail and an even split. Option 3, the band over a full-bleed photo, was rejected. No view is built yet. The five options are left here as the record of what was considered — this page is history now, not a live menu. The decisions and their reasoning are in docs/decisions/: the rail is paper because a reflective panel's chrome is dark on paper, and the accent ink on the event times is load-bearing rather than decorative.

What settled it was the real panel, not this drawing. Both rail colours were built for the Kitchen Counter display with a live calendar and published to the glass. Two numbers moved the decision, and neither is visible here: real event titles ran to 41 characters against 12 to 16 in the fixtures, and at 62 / 38 that fits 2 of a 6-event day against 6 of 6 at an even split. The panel drawings below still use the short fixture titles, so they make the narrow rail look more workable than it is.
